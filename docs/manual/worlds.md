# Worlds & game modes

This page explains how to find, join, and switch between worlds, and what a world's speed and
preset mean for how you play it.

## What is a world?

Eperica runs as a collection of **worlds** — separate maps, each with its own villages, players,
alliances, rankings, medals, and end-game timeline. Your account can participate in **multiple
worlds at once**, playing a different tribe in each one.

## The worlds lobby

After logging in you always land at the **worlds lobby** (`/worlds`), also reachable via the
**Worlds** link in the navigation bar. The lobby has two sections:

- **Your worlds** — worlds you have already joined. Your currently active world is highlighted.
  Click any of these to switch into it.
- **Joinable worlds** — running worlds you have not yet joined. Each entry shows the world's
  **name**, **speed** (e.g. 1×, 3×), and **size** (map radius). Click **Join** to enter.

## Joining a world

Your home-world tribe is chosen at registration. For every additional world in the joinable
list, select your **tribe** — Romans, Teutons, or Gauls — using the inline selector on the join
row, then click **Join**. This choice is **specific to that world**; you can play Gauls on one
world and Romans on another simultaneously.

The server creates your starting village on that world's map and redirects you to it. You are
now playing in that world.

## Switching between worlds

From the **worlds lobby** you can switch to any world you have already joined at any time. The
nav bar shows your current world and links back to the lobby so you never need to log out and
back in.

## World speed

Every world has a **speed multiplier** that controls the pace of play:

- **What scales with speed:** resource production, build times, troop movement, merchant travel,
  and all timers — everything goes faster on a higher-speed world.
- **What does not scale:** crop upkeep. Your troops and population eat the same fixed amount of
  crop per hour regardless of world speed. On a fast world your fields pour out crop much faster
  than the upkeep can consume it, so fast worlds are naturally **crop-abundant** — a different
  economic challenge than classic-speed play.

## Presets: classic and speed

Worlds are created with one of two **presets** that tune the balance rules independently of the
raw speed multiplier:

| Preset | Character |
|--------|-----------|
| **Classic** | The default Travian-faithful balance — standard beginner protection duration, standard troop movement base speeds, standard merchant travel. |
| **Speed** | Tweaked for a faster-paced experience: **shorter beginner protection**, **faster base troop movement**, and **faster merchant travel**. The world speed multiplier stacks on top of these adjustments. |

A world's preset is fixed at creation and does not change while the world is running.

## Isolation between worlds

Each world is fully independent:

- **Map** — your villages, oases, and coordinates exist only on that world's map.
- **Rankings & medals** — leaderboards and weekly medals are calculated per world.
- **Alliances** — an alliance on one world has no connection to an alliance with the same name on
  another world.
- **Resources & armies** — nothing transfers between worlds.

## Tips

- Try a speed world if you want a faster-paced game with shorter build and travel times.
- Your tribe choice on each world is permanent for that world — choose carefully based on the
  play style you want there.
- You can be active in several worlds at once; the lobby shows them all and tells you at a glance
  which one you last played.
