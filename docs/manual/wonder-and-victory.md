# The Wonder of the World & victory

Every round has an ending, and this is it: the round is won the moment an alliance completes the
**Wonder of the World**. Everything the artifact race built toward — the Treasuries, the fights over
Natar villages, the alliances forged under pressure — comes down to this last, grinding race to one
building.

## The second release

Some time after the artifacts appear, a second wave of Natar villages arrives, carrying two new
prizes:

- **Wonder plans** — held in Natar vaults, captured by force exactly like an artifact.
- **Wonder construction sites** — special Natar villages that, unlike the artifact vaults, **can be
  conquered**.

Until that date, neither exists; then both appear all at once, and the race is on.

## Capturing a plan

A Wonder plan is won the same way as a powerful artifact: attack from a village with a high-level
**Treasury** that doesn't already hold a plan, and beat whoever holds it — a Natar vault, or a rival
who captured one before you. The plan travels home with your victorious army either way.

## Taking a site

A Wonder site is a developed, heavily garrisoned Natar village — but unlike an artifact vault, it's
**conquerable**. Bring administrators, as you would to conquer any village, grind its loyalty to
zero, and the site becomes your alliance's.

## Raising the Wonder

Once your alliance holds a Wonder site **and** at least one plan, you can begin building the
**Wonder of the World** there. It rises one level at a time through the normal build queue, all the
way to **level 100** — each level costing more and taking longer than the last. This is not a solo
project: keep the site supplied and defended, because a determined rival can still attack it while
it climbs.

> **Tip:** Treat the site like your alliance's most valuable village, because it is — the Wonder's
> construction queue doesn't pause for a siege.

## Winning the round

The **first alliance to a complete, level-100 Wonder wins the round.** Victory is recorded the
instant it happens and can never be taken away by a later finisher — there's no second place that
becomes first.

When the world is won, it **freezes**: the result is final, and game actions stop everywhere. You
can still log in and look around — the standings page shows the final table and crowns the winner,
and your own village carries the victory notice — but the fighting is over.

Well played.
