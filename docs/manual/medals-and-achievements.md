# Medals & achievements — prestige

Beyond the live leaderboards, the world records lasting prestige: **medals** for topping a weekly
category, and **achievements** for reaching milestones.

## How weekly medals settle

Once a week — every 7 days of real time, regardless of world speed — the server takes a snapshot of
everyone's population and runs a **settlement**, awarding **medals** to that week's top performers
in each category:

- **Top attacker / defender / raider** — most attack points, defence points, and resources looted
  during the week.
- **Top climber** — most population **gained** over the week (the snapshot is what makes this
  possible).
- **Top alliances** — by aggregate member population, and by aggregate attack/defence points.

The top few in each category earn a medal recording the category, rank, and week. Medals are
**permanent** — they stay on your profile forever and accumulate week after week. Your medals
appear on your statistics page; alliance medals appear on the alliance's page.

## Earning achievements

**Achievements** are one-time badges for crossing a milestone. The starter set:

- Found a second village
- Win 100 defensive battles
- Occupy your first oasis
- Reach 1,000 population
- Research every unit of your tribe

Each is granted automatically the first time you meet it — once only — and some carry a small
**reward**, culture points or resources delivered straight to your capital. Your earned
achievements show on your statistics page.

> **Tip:** Watch your **Top Climbers** ranking and **population-over-time** chart on the
> [leaderboards](ranking-and-statistics.md) page — both come from the same weekly snapshot that
> powers medals.
