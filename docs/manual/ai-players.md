# AI players (NPCs)

Some worlds are populated by AI-controlled players. This page explains what they are and how they
appear to you.

## What AI players are

**AI players** are accounts controlled by a bot rather than a human. They build villages, train
troops, raid, settle, and appear on rankings, leaderboards, and the map just like any other player.
For gameplay purposes they're full participants in the world: you can attack them, raid them, send
them messages, and even encounter them as members of alliances.

## Labeled vs disguised worlds

How visible an AI player is depends on the world's setting:

- **Labeled worlds** — AI players carry an **"NPC" tag** on their profile page, on leaderboard rows
  that name them, and in the map inspector for their villages. You'll always know you're dealing
  with a bot.
- **Disguised worlds** — AI players are **indistinguishable from humans** everywhere a player looks:
  profile, leaderboard entries, battle reports, messages, and map presence all look identical to a
  human's. The world listing may or may not tell you AI players are present; on a truly disguised
  world you'll need to use your own judgment.

> **Faithful:** This setting is fixed for each world by the operator and doesn't change while the
> world runs.

## How they behave in the world

AI players are full participants:

- They build, raid, settle, and train troops using the normal game systems.
- They appear in [rankings and medal boards](ranking-and-statistics.md) and can earn weekly medals.
- They can be attacked, raided, and conquered exactly like human villages.
- They receive [beginner protection](protection-and-lifecycle.md) at the start of the world, just
  as new human players do.
- They can be members of alliances, or found their own.

## Reporting an AI player

On a disguised world, if you suspect a player is an AI, you can **report them** just as you would
any player. Moderators reviewing the report can see the account's true status and judge
accordingly. Filing a report on an AI player on a disguised world doesn't break the disguise for
you — moderators handle it behind the scenes.

## When an AI player goes quiet

An AI player that's actually playing counts as active exactly like a human — its play refreshes the
same activity signal, so it doesn't show the map's greyed "inactive" marker, and the abandonment
sweep never removes it. An AI player whose access has been withdrawn goes quiet and is treated like
any quiet player: it greys out and is eventually swept from the map through the normal lifecycle.
