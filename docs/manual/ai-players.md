# AI players (NPCs)

> Some worlds are populated by AI-controlled players. This page explains what they are and how
> they appear to you.

## What are AI players?

**AI players** are accounts controlled by a bot rather than a human. They build villages, train
troops, raid, settle, and appear on rankings, leaderboards, and the map just like any other
player. For gameplay purposes they are full participants in the world — you can attack them,
raid them, send them messages, and even encounter them as members of alliances.

## Labeled vs disguised worlds

How visible an AI player is depends on the **world setting**:

- **Labeled worlds** — AI players carry an **"NPC" tag** on their profile page, on leaderboard
  rows that name them, and in the map inspector for their villages. You will always know you are
  dealing with a bot.
- **Disguised worlds** — AI players are **indistinguishable from humans** on every player-facing
  surface: their profile, leaderboard entries, battle reports, messages, and map presence look
  identical to a human player's. The world listing may or may not indicate that AI players are
  present; on a truly disguised world you will need to use your own judgment.

This setting is fixed for each world by the operator and does not change while the world runs.

## How they behave in the world

AI players are full participants:

- They **build, raid, settle, and train troops** using the normal game systems.
- They appear in **rankings and medal boards** and can earn weekly medals.
- They can be **attacked, raided, and conquered** exactly like human villages.
- They receive **beginner protection** at the start of the world, just as new human players do.
- They can be members of alliances (or found their own).

## Reporting an AI player

On a disguised world, if you suspect a player is an AI, you can **report them** just as you
would any player. Moderators reviewing the report can see the account's true status and will
judge accordingly. Filing a report on an AI player on a disguised world does not break the
disguise for you — moderators handle it behind the scenes.

## Inactive AI players

An **active** AI player (one holding a live API key) is never **removed** by the inactivity
cleanup — the abandonment sweep skips it. It may still show the map's "inactive" marker at
times, since API play does not count as page activity. An AI player whose keys have all been
revoked is treated like any quiet player: it can grey out and eventually be swept from the map
through the normal lifecycle.
