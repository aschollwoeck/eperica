# Protection & a living world

A new village is fragile, and an MMO map clogged with dead villages goes stale. Two systems keep
the start fair and the world fresh.

## Beginner's protection

When you register, your village is **immune to attack** for a protection window — shorter on
faster worlds, since everything else moves faster too. No one can raid or attack you while you find
your feet; your village page shows how much protection remains.

Protection is a foothold, not a fortress. It ends early the moment either of these happens:

- you **grow up** — once your population passes a threshold, you're established and protection
  lifts on its own; or
- you **go on the offensive** — the instant you launch your own attack or raid, your protection
  ends. You can't hit others while hiding behind it.

> **Warning:** Once protection ends, it doesn't come back — pick your first fight deliberately.

## Inactivity & abandonment

The world reclaims players who stop playing, in two stages:

1. **Inactive (farmable).** After a stretch with no activity, an account is marked **inactive** —
   its villages show **greyed** on the [map](the-map.md) so active players can spot them as farms.
   An inactive player can still be attacked under the normal rules; nothing else changes for them.
2. **Abandoned.** After a much longer absence, the account is **retired**: its villages are removed
   from the map, freeing those valleys for new settlement, and the account can no longer log in.

This keeps the map alive and reclaimable instead of frozen around players who have left. Staying
active — even just logging in — keeps your account out of the lifecycle entirely. Beginner
protection scales with world speed, but the inactivity and abandonment windows are real wall-clock
time regardless of speed.

## AI players and Natar accounts

**AI-controlled players** count as active while they're actually playing — their moves refresh the
same activity signal a human's do, so a playing AI account neither greys out nor gets swept. Once
it stops playing, it re-enters the normal lifecycle like any quiet player: greyed first, then
eventually removed. See [AI players](ai-players.md) for what else to expect from them.

**Natar accounts** — the synthetic accounts that hold end-game artifact and Wonder villages — sit
outside this system entirely: excluded from the abandonment sweep and from the leaderboards. Their
villages may still carry the map's "inactive" marker, since that marker is derived purely from
activity, not from what kind of account it is.
