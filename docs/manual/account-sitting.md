# Account sitting

Going away? A trusted friend can keep your account running while you're gone — **account sitting**.
Find it under **Sitting** in the top bar.

## Authorising a sitter

On the Sitting page, add a player by username under **Your sitters**. This is a trust decision: a
sitter can operate your account exactly as you would — build, train, send troops, trade, message —
but they **cannot**:

- change your settings or your profile,
- manage your sitters, or
- hand your account to someone else.

You can **revoke** a sitter at any time. If they're operating your account at that moment, they're
dropped back to their own immediately.

## Sitting for someone else

If another player has authorised you, they appear under **Accounts you sit for**. Click **Sit** to
take over — a banner across the top reminds you whose account you're operating, with a
**Stop sitting** button to return to your own. While sitting, everything you do happens as the
owner, under their name.

## The audit log

Everything a sitter does on an account is recorded under **Recent sitter activity** on the owner's
Sitting page — who did what, and when, so the trust you extend is verifiable rather than blind.

> **Warning:** Sitting can't be used to dodge a ban. A suspended or banned player can't sit, and a
> suspended or banned account can't be operated by a sitter. The usual rules — and the end-of-round
> freeze — apply throughout.
