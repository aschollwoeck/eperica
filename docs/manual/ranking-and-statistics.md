# Ranking, leaderboards & statistics — the scoreboard

The world keeps score. **Leaderboards** rank everyone by how big they are and how hard they fight,
and every player and alliance has a public **statistics** page. Rankings are open to all — you
don't need to be logged in to read them.

## The metrics

- **Population** — your size, from the levels of your resource fields and buildings, summed across
  **all** your villages. It's the headline ranking and the public measure of how big a player is.
- **Attack points** — earned by destroying enemy troops on the offensive. The more, and the more
  valuable, the troops you kill while attacking, the more you score.
- **Defence points** — earned by killing attackers while defending. When several players defend one
  village together, the points **split between them in proportion to how much defence each
  brought** — reinforcing an ally is rewarded, not just defending your own village.
- **Loot** — the resources you've hauled home as a raider.

Points are awarded when the battle happens and never change afterward, even if the game's balance
is later tuned — your record is your record.

## Reading the leaderboards

The **Leaderboards** page, linked at the top of every page, has a tab per category:

- **Population** — players by total population.
- **Top attackers / defenders / raiders** — players by attack points, defence points, and loot.
- **Alliances** — alliances by aggregate member population, and by aggregate attack/defence points.

Two filters refine every board:

- **Region** — the whole world, or one **quadrant** (NE / NW / SE / SW), decided by your capital's
  location.
- **Window** — for the attacker / defender / raider boards, rank over all time or just the last 7
  or 30 days, so a current burst of activity shows even against long-established players.

## Reading a statistics page

Click any name on a leaderboard to open its **statistics page**: a player's total population (with
a per-village breakdown), attack/defence points, and loot; or an alliance's aggregates and its
member roster. These pages show only public numbers — a rival's exact army and stored resources
stay hidden.

> **Tip:** When you reinforce an ally and their village is attacked, you get your **own** battle
> report in your reports inbox, showing what happened to your troops and the defence points you
> earned — not the defender's full battle.

## Medals, achievements & top climbers

Players earn permanent **medals** (weekly population prizes, milestone trophies) and
**achievements** for reaching key milestones, some with resource and culture rewards. A
**Top Climbers** board shows who grew fastest in the last week, and your **population-over-time**
chart tracks your village history. See [Medals & achievements](medals-and-achievements.md) for the
full breakdown.
