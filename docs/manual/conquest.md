# Conquest — take an enemy's village

Settling founds villages on empty land — the peaceful way to grow. **Conquest** is the aggressive
one: with the right troops you can take an enemy's village for yourself, buildings, fields,
resources and all. The key is **loyalty**, the **administrator** unit, and a free expansion slot.

## Loyalty

Every village has a **loyalty** score from 0 to 100. A fully loyal village (100) is firmly held; as
loyalty falls it wavers, and at **0** it can change hands. Check any of your villages' loyalty in
the **Culture & expansion** panel on its village page.

Loyalty **regenerates on its own**, back toward 100 at **2 points per hour** (faster on
speed-scaled worlds). An attacker who chips away at your loyalty but can't finish the job will see
it recover — conquest takes sustained pressure, not one lucky hit.

## Administrators

Each tribe has an **administrator** unit — the Roman **Senator**, the Teuton **Chief**, the Gaul
**Chieftain**. They train in the Residence or Palace after a steep research requirement, they're
slow and expensive, and — unlike settlers — they fight in the battle. Their real job starts after
the fighting: each administrator that **survives a winning attack** knocks the target's loyalty
down by **20–30 points** (a random roll, independent for each survivor). Send several and each gets
its own roll — the reductions add together. A freshly conquered village resets to **25 loyalty**,
so it can't be instantly re-taken by someone else.

## Taking a village

1. **Win the battle.** Send a strong attack — administrators alone won't beat a defended village —
   from the [Rally Point](troop-movement.md), with your administrators riding along.
2. **Survive with an administrator.** If the attack wins and at least one administrator lives, it
   strikes the target's loyalty.
3. **Grind loyalty to zero.** One attack usually isn't enough; repeat until loyalty hits 0 before
   it can regenerate back up.
4. **Have a free expansion slot.** Taking a village is an expansion, exactly like settling — you
   need the culture points and Residence/Palace capacity to hold it. With no free slot you can
   still grind loyalty down, but you can't take the village until you make room.

When loyalty reaches 0 and you have a free slot, the village becomes yours — fields, buildings, and
stored resources intact. Its garrison is cleared, and it appears in your village switcher
immediately, running under your control. The previous owner loses it on the spot.

> **Faithful:** A player's **capital** — the village holding their Palace — can never be conquered.
> Administrators reduce its loyalty by nothing, so choosing where to plant your own capital is a
> real strategic decision, not just a formality.

## Reports and defence

Every attack carrying administrators produces a **battle report** showing loyalty before and
after, and — when it happens — "Village captured!" Both attacker and defender see it.

To defend against conquest:

- **Keep loyalty high** — it regenerates, so a well-defended village recovers between attacks.
- **Build a Palace** to make your most important village an unconquerable capital.
- **Garrison and reinforce** — administrators only strike after a winning attack, so a strong
  defence stops conquest cold. See [Attacking & defending](combat.md) for how battles resolve.
