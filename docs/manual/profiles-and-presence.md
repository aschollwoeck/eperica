# Your profile & who's online

Eperica gives you a little identity and shows you who's around.

## Writing your bio

Open **Profile** from the top bar. There you can write a short **bio** — plain text, up to 500
characters — that appears on your public statistics page for other players to read. Edit it any
time and click **Save**. Only you can change your own bio.

## Reading presence — online or last seen

Next to a player's name you'll see a small presence marker:

- **● online** — they were active just now, within the last few minutes.
- **○ last seen …** — how long ago they were last active, in minutes, hours, or days.

You'll find it in the places where you already look at other players: their public stats page, the
[leaderboards](ranking-and-statistics.md), your conversations list and the header of a
direct-message thread, and on [map](the-map.md) village markers, in the tile's label.

> **Faithful:** Presence is just a hint about activity — it never reveals anything private about an
> account. Your troops, resources, and the like always stay hidden. Group channels like Global and
> Alliance don't show a single presence, since many players share them.
