# Tribes, the Academy & the Smithy

> This page covers the three tribes, every unit in the game, and how to research and upgrade them.

## Choosing your tribe

You choose your **first tribe at registration** — that becomes your tribe for the home world.
Every additional world you join from the lobby gets its own tribe choice; you pick it inline on
the join row before pressing Join. Each world you play in can have its own tribe. The three
tribes are:

- **Romans** — balanced, high-quality troops. More expensive and slower to train than the
  others, but they have the strongest cavalry in the late game and a unique economic advantage:
  Roman builders can work on a **resource field and a centre building at the same time** while
  everyone else builds one project at a time.
- **Teutons** — cheap, fast-to-train troops. The weakest defenders early on, but the strongest
  **early raiders** and the most crop-efficient soldiers in the game. Their Scout trains in the
  **Barracks** (not the Stable) — a foot scout rather than a mounted one.
- **Gauls** — **defensive specialists** with the fastest cavalry in the game. The Theutates
  Thunder (speed 19) is the quickest mounted unit of any tribe. Recommended for new players
  because of their strong defensive profile.

## Unit roster overview

Each tribe has **ten units**. Your tribe's **tier-1 infantry** and **Settlers** do not need
Academy research — they are available as soon as the relevant building is built. All other units
must be researched in the Academy before they can be trained.

## Researching units (Academy)

1. Build the prerequisites: **Main Building 3** → **Barracks** → **Barracks 3** → **Academy**.
2. Open the **Academy** from your village page.
3. Choose a unit and press **Research**. Resources are spent immediately; when the countdown
   finishes the unit is available to train.

Only one research runs at a time per village. Some units also require the Stable or Workshop —
their exact requirements are listed in the tables below.

## Upgrading units (Smithy)

The **Smithy** (needs Main Building 3 + Academy 1) raises a unit's combat strength level by
level:

1. Open the **Smithy**.
2. Press **Upgrade** on a researched unit to raise it one level.

A unit's level can never exceed the **Smithy's own building level**. One upgrade runs
independently of research and construction.

defining cavalry unit — the Teutonic Knight at attack 150 — is among the strongest attackers

unit in the game (def-inf 115), and their Haeduan hits hard. The **Theutates Thunder** is the

## Tips

- Tier-1 units (Legionnaire / Clubswinger / Phalanx) and **Settlers** need no Academy research —
  they are ready to train as soon as you have the Barracks or Residence.
- Research units before you need them; research takes time and blocks the Academy lane.
- Administrators (Senator / Chief / Chieftain) and Settlers both train in the **Residence** (or
  Palace). You need at least Rally Point level 5 and Academy level 10 before you can research
  the administrator.
- Gauls wanting a quick reinforcement force should prioritise the **Theutates Thunder** — nothing
  else on the map arrives faster.
- Teutons: your Scout is unusually cheap (only 160/100/50/50) and trains in the Barracks, so
  you can field scouts well before you have a Stable.
