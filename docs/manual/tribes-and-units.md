# Tribes, the Academy & the Smithy

Every village fights with one of three tribes, each with its own units, strengths, and a
distinct economic quirk. This page covers choosing a tribe and how to research and upgrade your
units — full unit stats and costs live in the generated reference, linked below.

## Choosing your tribe

You choose your **first tribe at registration** — that becomes your tribe for the home world.
Every additional world you join from the lobby gets its own tribe choice; you pick it inline on
the join row before pressing Join. Each world you play in can have its own tribe. The three
tribes are:

- **Romans** — balanced, high-quality troops. More expensive and slower to train than the
  others, but their **Equites Caesaris** is the hardest-hitting cavalry unit in the game, and
  their unique economic advantage — the **dual build queue** — lets a Roman work on a resource
  field and a centre building **at the same time**, while everyone else builds one project at a
  time.
- **Teutons** — cheap, fast-to-train troops. The weakest defenders early on, but the strongest
  **early raiders** and the most crop-efficient soldiers in the game. Their **Teutonic Knight**
  (attack 150) hits harder per resource spent than almost anything else on the map. Their Scout
  trains in the **Barracks**, not the Stable — a foot scout rather than a mounted one, and
  unusually cheap to field early.
- **Gauls** — **defensive specialists** with the fastest cavalry in the game. Their
  **Druidrider** is the best defensive cavalry unit around, and the **Theutates Thunder** (speed
  19) is the quickest mounted unit of any tribe — the kings of rapid reinforcement. Recommended
  for new players for their strong defensive profile.

## Researching units (Academy)

1. Build the prerequisites: **Main Building 3** → **Barracks** → **Barracks 3** → **Academy**.
2. Open the **Academy** from your village page.
3. Choose a unit and press **Research**. Resources are spent immediately; when the countdown
   finishes the unit is available to train.

Only one research runs at a time per village. Your tribe's **tier-1 infantry** and **Settlers**
need no research at all — they're ready as soon as the Barracks or Residence is built. Some
units also need the Stable or Workshop first; see the reference for exact requirements.

## Upgrading units (Smithy)

The **Smithy** (needs Main Building 3 + Academy 1) raises a unit's combat strength level by
level:

1. Open the **Smithy**.
2. Press **Upgrade** on a researched unit to raise it one level.

A unit's level can never exceed the **Smithy's own building level**. Upgrades run independently
of research and construction.

## Tips

- Tier-1 units (Legionnaire / Clubswinger / Phalanx) and **Settlers** need no Academy research —
  they're ready to train as soon as you have the Barracks or Residence.
- Research units before you need them; research takes time and blocks the Academy lane.
- Administrators (Senator / Chief / Chieftain) and Settlers both train in the **Residence** (or
  Palace); you need Rally Point level 5 and Academy level 10 before you can research the
  administrator.
- Gauls wanting a quick reinforcement force should prioritise the **Theutates Thunder** — nothing
  else on the map arrives faster.
- Teutons: your Scout is unusually cheap and trains in the Barracks, so you can field scouts well
  before you have a Stable.
