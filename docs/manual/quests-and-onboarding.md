# Quests & onboarding — your first steps

A new village starts slow. To get you going, the game gives you a short **guided quest chain** that
walks you through the core loop and **rewards** you for each step — resources and culture points.

## How quests work

Quests come **one at a time**, in order. You always have one **current** quest; finishing it unlocks the
next. You can't skip ahead — the next quest only appears once you've completed the one before it.

Each quest **completes automatically** the first time you do its action — there's no "claim" button.
The server checks your village whenever you load it (and the Quests page), so the moment you meet a
quest's goal, it completes and its reward lands. If you've done several steps already (say you came back
after a while), they all complete in order the next time you check in.

## The starter chain

The onboarding chain walks you through:

1. **Upgrade a resource field** to level 2 — reward: a bundle of resources.
2. **Build a Warehouse** — reward: more resources.
3. **Train your first troops** — reward: culture points.
4. **Send a raid** against another village — reward: resources.
5. **Grow your village** to 50 population — reward: culture points.

Resource rewards are delivered to your **capital** (up to its storage capacity); culture point
rewards go straight to your account.

## Where to see them

Open **Quests** from the village menu. You'll see your **current** quest with its reward, and the list
of quests you've already **completed**. Quests are private — only you see your own.

## It ends

The chain is **finite**. Once you've finished the last quest, there are no more — you've learned the
game, and from there your goals come from the world itself: growing, fighting, allying, and conquering.
