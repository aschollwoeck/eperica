# Eperica — Player Manual

Welcome to Eperica, a medieval strategy world of villages, resources, armies, and alliances. This
manual grows as the game does.

## Contents

### 1. Getting started

- [Getting started](getting-started.md) — create your account, join a world, and take your first
  steps in your village.
- [Worlds & game modes](worlds.md) — the worlds lobby, joining and switching worlds, speed
  multipliers, and the classic and speed presets.
- [Quests & onboarding](quests-and-onboarding.md) — a guided starter quest chain that walks you
  through the core loop and rewards each step.
- [The world map](the-map.md) — read the map, find valleys and oases, and get around.

### 2. Economy

- [Resources](resources.md) — how your village produces and stores wood, clay, iron, and crop;
  upkeep and the crop rule on fast worlds.
- [Building & upgrading](buildings.md) — the village's 22 slots, the build queue, and what every
  building is for.
- [Trading](trade.md) — build a Marketplace and send resources to other villages via merchants.

### 3. Military

- [Tribes, the Academy & the Smithy](tribes-and-units.md) — choose your tribe per world and
  research and upgrade units.
- [Training troops & feeding your army](training-and-upkeep.md) — train batches at the Barracks,
  Stable, and Workshop, and keep your army fed.
- [Moving troops](troop-movement.md) — send reinforcements from the Rally Point, watch movements
  arrive, and recall your troops home.
- [Attacking & defending](combat.md) — launch attacks and raids, build a Wall, and read battle
  reports.
- [Scouting](scouting.md) — send scouts to reveal an enemy's resources or defenses, and counter
  enemy spies.
- [Siege & loot](siege-and-loot.md) — raid for resources, smash buildings with catapults, and
  protect your stores with a Cranny.
- [Oases](oases.md) — clear the wild animals, occupy oases through an Outpost, and hold them for a
  production bonus.

### 4. Expansion

- [Settling](settling.md) — build culture points, train settlers, found new villages on free
  valleys, and designate your capital with a Palace.
- [Conquest](conquest.md) — lower an enemy village's loyalty with administrators and take it for
  your own; keep your capital safe.
- [Protection & a living world](protection-and-lifecycle.md) — beginner's protection from attack
  at the start, and how inactive accounts become farmable and eventually free up the map.

### 5. Society

- [Alliances & diplomacy](alliances.md) — build an Embassy, found or join an alliance, hold roles
  & rights, and defend together with shared visibility and incoming-attack warnings.
- [The alliance forum](alliance-forum.md) — your alliance's private threaded discussion: start
  threads, reply, and post locked announcements.
- [Communication — messages & chat](communication.md) — direct messages and live global/alliance
  chat channels, with unread badges.
- [Notifications & alerts](notifications.md) — the nav bell: live, private alerts for incoming
  attacks, battle reports, and new messages.
- [Your profile & who's online](profiles-and-presence.md) — write a public bio and see other
  players' online / last-seen presence.
- [Finding players, alliances & places](search.md) — the search box: jump to a player, an alliance,
  or a map tile by coordinate.
- [Settings & preferences](settings.md) — your own preferences, starting with per-kind
  notification controls.
- [Account sitting](account-sitting.md) — let a trusted player operate your account while you're
  away, within limits and with a full audit log.
- [Ranking & statistics](ranking-and-statistics.md) — leaderboards, alliance aggregates, public
  stat pages, and the points you earn defending allies.
- [Medals & achievements](medals-and-achievements.md) — weekly medals, one-time milestone
  achievements, the top-climbers board, and population over time.
- [Fair play & moderation](fair-play-and-moderation.md) — reporting cheats, sanctions, and the
  moderator tools that keep a world clean.
- [AI players (NPCs)](ai-players.md) — bot players in the world: labeled vs disguised, and what
  happens when they retire.
- [Spectating](spectating.md) — the admin-granted, read-only observer role and its live-feed
  dashboard.

### 6. Reference

- [Artifacts & the Natars](artifacts.md) — the end-game's first phase: artifacts released into
  Natar villages, captured (and stolen) by force from a Treasury village.
- [The Wonder of the World & victory](wonder-and-victory.md) — the end-game's finale: capture
  Wonder plans, conquer a Wonder site, and build the Wonder to win the round.
- [Full unit stats](/manual/reference/units) — every tribe's stats, costs, upkeep, and
  prerequisites. Live stats, always current.
- [All buildings & prerequisites](/manual/reference/buildings) — purpose, prerequisites, and
  per-level values for every building. Live stats, always current.
- [The numbers](/manual/reference/mechanics) — culture thresholds, expansion slots, loyalty,
  walls & siege durability, merchants, and more. Live stats, always current.
