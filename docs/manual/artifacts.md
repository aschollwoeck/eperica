# Artifacts & the Natars — the end-game begins

A world is a finite round, and every round ends the same way it began: with a fight. The end-game
opens when the wild **Natar** tribe rises from the map's reserved tiles, each of their villages
guarding a single, powerful **artifact**. This is the first act of a story that closes with the
Wonder of the World — and it changes what "winning" means for everyone still standing.

## The release

At a set date, the Natar villages materialize on the map all at once. Until then, there are no
Natars and no artifacts to speak of — the moment the date turns, they're simply there, guarding
their prize, waiting to be fought over.

## What artifacts do

Every artifact has a **type** and a **reach**. The types:

- **Speed** — your troops march faster.
- **Storage** — bigger warehouse and granary capacity.
- **Sustenance** — cheaper troop upkeep.
- **Trainer** — faster training.
- **Architect** — buildings tougher against catapults.
- **Eyes** — sharper scouting.
- **Confuser** — harder for others to scout you.
- **Fool** — chaotic; it behaves like one of the others, decided when it's released.

The reach decides how far the bonus spreads:

- **Small** — just the village holding it.
- **Large** — every village you own.
- **Unique** — every village you own, and it's the strongest version of its kind on the world.

The bonus lasts exactly as long as you hold the artifact, and vanishes the instant you lose it —
there's no lingering benefit from an artifact that's slipped from your grasp.

## Taking an artifact

Holding an artifact takes a **Treasury** — build one, and a bigger artifact simply needs a higher
Treasury level. A village can hold only one artifact at a time.

You **capture** an artifact by winning an attack, launched from your Treasury village, against the
Natar village guarding it — the artifact comes home in your victorious army's baggage. The same
move works against a rival: beat a player who holds an artifact, attacking from your own Treasury
village, and you **steal** it out from under them.

> **Warning:** Natar villages are tough, developed targets, not pushovers — bring a real army.
> They can't be conquered or owned outright; you're fighting for the artifact inside, not the land
> underneath it.

## Where to look

Natar villages show on the map, and your held artifacts — type, reach, effect, and which village
holds each — appear on your village view. Artifact locations are public knowledge; expect every one
of them to be contested from the moment the Natars arrive.
