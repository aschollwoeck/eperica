# Communication — messages & chat

Eperica has one place to talk to other players: **Messages**. It works like a modern chat app — a
list of conversations, each a running thread that updates live.

## Reading your conversations

Open **Messages** from the top bar. You'll see your conversations, newest first: your
**direct-message** threads with other players, plus the **group channels** you can see — a
**Global** channel everyone shares, and your **Alliance** channel if you're in one. Each row shows
the latest line and an unread count; the nav badge shows your total unread.

## Sending a direct message

Open a player's profile — from a leaderboard row or a stats page — and click **Message**. That
opens your conversation with them. Type in the box and send; both of you see the same running
thread, with new lines appearing live. Opening a conversation marks it read.

## Channels

- **Global** — open to every player.
- **Alliance** — only members of your alliance can read or post in it.

Posting to a channel you're not allowed in is refused by the server.

## Live updates

Messages appear in an open conversation within about a second, without reloading. Under the hood
they're delivered over a live stream; if your connection drops, the full history is still there
when you reload — nothing is lost.

## Rules

Everything goes through the server: you can only read your own conversations and the channels
you're allowed in. Sending is **rate-limited** so no one can flood a channel, and an account that's
suspended or banned for a fair-play violation can't send (though it can still read).

> **Warning:** See something abusive? Report the player from their profile — moderators review
> reports and can sanction. See [Fair play & moderation](fair-play-and-moderation.md).
