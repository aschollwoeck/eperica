Protection & a living world

Beginner's protection lasts {{ protection_base }} (base — this scales down with the world's speed, like every other duration on this site) or until you reach {{ protection_population_threshold }} population, whichever comes first.

An account greys out on the map (farmable) after {{ inactive_after }} with no login, and is abandoned — its villages freed back to the map — after {{ abandon_after }}. Both are real wall-clock time, not scaled by world speed: a faster world does not grey or abandon idle players any sooner.
